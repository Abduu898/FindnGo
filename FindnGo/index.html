Need to know

Questions, answered.

What do I need to rent a car?

Bring a valid driving license, a form of ID, and the booking details we confirm with you. Our partner may request a security deposit.

Can I pick up in one city and return in another?

Yes. One-way trips are available with selected partners. Add your route in the request and we will confirm the options and any fee.

Are the cars inspected?

Every listed vehicle comes from a verified local partner and is checked before handoff. We make the car condition clear before you commit.
